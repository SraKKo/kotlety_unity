PSX Post-Soviet Yard - sampler v0.1
17 low-poly PS1-style assets: modular 5-storey panel building pieces (3.2 x 2.8 m grid),
entrance, glazed balcony, kiosk, bus stop, garage, yellow gas pipes, bench, trash container,
carpet beater, pull-up bar, street lamp, asphalt tile.
12-180 triangles per asset, 128px nearest-filtered textures with baked grime.
1 unit = 1 m, origin on the floor, front faces -Y (Blender) / +Z (GLB).
Formats: GLB (Godot/Unity/Unreal/Blender), FBX (all assets, embedded textures), .blend source.
